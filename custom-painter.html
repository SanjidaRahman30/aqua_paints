🎨 How to Use the Custom Room Visualizer

Why this feature? Unlike preset rooms, this allows you to upload a picture of your own real walls and test how Aqua Paints shades will look in your home lighting before you buy paint.

1
Upload or Pick a Room Photo Click Change Photo to upload a photo of your bedroom, living room, or exterior from your device, or pick any ready sample room.
2
Pick an Aqua Paints Color Browse all 294 colors on the right palette or search by code (e.g. AS01, AS79) or color name.
3
Paint Walls with Smooth Brush or Smart Fill Drag the Smooth Brush across your wall to paint, or tap with Smart Fill to automatically color a wall section! Use Soft Eraser to tidy frames and trim.
4
Save & Download Click Download Visualizer Photo to save your room preview with the official Aqua Paints color label.
